Om AMITEC

Amitec is a European consulting and technology company helping industrial businesses turn PROCESS DATA into useful INSIGHTS.

Since 1994, we have empowered organizations with innovative solutions, applications, and expertise to bridge the gap between operational technology and information technology. Our mission is to enable faster, smarter business decisions based on accurate, accessible, and trusted process data.

For decades, we have worked with the major players in the oil and gas industry and have extensive experience supporting customers in chemicals, pharmaceuticals, wind and hydro power, manufacturing, and infrastructure.
