Agens ↓ Work → About → Contact → Privacy ↑ Privacy Agens AS, org.nr. 988 669 695, Trondheimsveien 184, 0570 Oslo, is responsible for the personal data on this website. Ask us anything about it at post@agens.no.

This site sets no cookies and keeps nothing in your browser that could recognise you. We count visits with PostHog, on its servers in the EU, in a mode that stores nothing on your device. To tell one day's visits apart, PostHog combines your IP address and browser details with a random value that changes every day, and keeps only the scrambled result. It cannot follow you from one day to the next or to any other site, and we never connect it to your name. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in knowing which pages people read. Your browser also keeps the scroll position it restores when you go back a page, and discards it the moment you close the tab.

Loading a page reaches three machines: our host, Vercel; Sanity, which stores and serves the images; and PostHog, which counts the visit. All three see what any web server sees, including your IP address, because that is how a page gets to you at all.
