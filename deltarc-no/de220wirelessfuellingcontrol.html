DE220WirelessFuellingControl

An overview of the products we offer for the aviation and road fuelling industry.

The DE220 Wireless Fuelling Control System by Delta RC is the next-generation solution for safe, efficient, and reliable aircraft fuelling . With significant advancements over the DE212, it ensures better safety, reduced operational costs, and enhanced scalability .

This system is the third generation of the Delta wireless Deadman control system, designed for aero plane refuelling trucks, hydrant dispensers and road trucks.

The Delta DE212 FailSafe RC is a rugged and sturdy, purpose-built wired deadman control for ground refuelling trucks and dispensers.
