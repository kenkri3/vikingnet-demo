Om DELTA RC

We develop wireless remote controls today with tomorrows technology customised for aviation, construction and assistant markets and we have the worlds first EX-certified wireless deadman remote control.

Delta RC AS delivers Ex/ATEX approved solutions for aviation ground refuelling for trucks and dispensers. Our product range includes a wired option in addition to our most sold item – the wireless dead man control.

Our rugged and sturdy products for construction can be used in different industries such as dig sites, trucks as well as cranes and other remote controlled equipment.

Mer om det vi gjør

We make remote controls for the assistant-market such as HC lifts, sun screen solutions as well as other helpful gadgets. Most of our assistant-remote controls are sold through our partners in Norway – Handicare AS and TekTeam AS.

Founded in 1986 in Sandefjord, Norway, Delta RC has been dedicated to designing and manufacturing high-quality wireless remote control systems for demanding industrial applications.
