Om FALCK NORGE

Since 1906, Falck has grown with the same ambition: to turn care into action. Watch the film to see how Falck has evolved from the very beginning to the work we do today.

As a global team of experts, we use our wide knowledge, extensive healthcare and emergency response expertise, and rapidly evolving technology to care for people in need. Regardless of your tasks, you will be part of our mission to help people feel safe and live healthier lives.
