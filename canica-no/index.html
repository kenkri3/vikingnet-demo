Creating value through excellence! About Canica Canica is a privately owned investment company, operating out of Norway and Switzerland.

Canica consists of various holding structures with multiple group companies and invests in a range of different sectors mainly divided into three main categories: industrial investments, financial investments and real estate. Canica has a global presence, focused on the Nordics, selected countries in Central Europe and the United States.
