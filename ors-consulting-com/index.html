Om ORS CONSULTING

Risk Management and Process Safety Advisory for your business ORS Consulting (ORS) was established in 2009 as an independent and specialist provider of risk management advisory services.

Our ultimate goal is to ensure that our Clients avoid loss and achieve enhanced value creation.

Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA) is widely used to evaluate major accident hazards across process industries. However, facilities handling energetic materials such as explosives, propellants and ammunition require specialised methodologies that differ from conventional Process QRA.
