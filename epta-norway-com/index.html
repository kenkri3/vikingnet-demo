Om EPTA REFRIGERATION NORWAY

Product, technology, and service solutions for the preservation and presentation of fresh and frozen products.

Services A comprehensive range of services, from store and refrigeration line design and planning to after-sales service, maintenance, remote monitoring, and cabinet modernization.

Epta Norway Partner Chain Epta Partner chain is a nationwide chain of refrigeration contractors. Epta Partner chain has more than 250 competent professionals who design, build and maintain refrigeration systems for customers all over the country.
