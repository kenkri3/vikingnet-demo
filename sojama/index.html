Services

Sourcing for third-party equipment.

Four decades as an OEM have given us an extensive global network for sourcing electrical, automation and hydraulic components, as well as casted parts for windlasses, capstans and gearwheels.

We support ship and rig owners in sourcing components and spare parts for third-party equipment — from mechanical components to hydraulics and electrical systems.

About SOJAMA

Your partner for global direct sourcing.

SOJAMA specialises in custom-made equipment for our clients, with the goal of taking full responsibility for the entire deck machinery package.

We combine cutting-edge technology with highly competitive products through an extensive global network of subcontractors — and for larger crane ranges we build as close to the yards and customers as possible, saving both delivery time and shipping costs.
