Om TELEPLAN CONSULTING

The Teleplan Group have a long history. We are proud of our abilities to innovate, to stay at the forefront of technology, to bring value to our customers and adapt to frequent changes.

Teleplan Globe Teleplan Globe develop and deliver solutions to Norwegian and international customers in the Defense, Homeland security and Telecom sectors. For more than six decades, the Teleplan Group of companies has built a strong legacy of innovation and adaptability.

Visit Teleplan Globe Teleplan Consulting Teleplan Consulting contributes to value creation in society by ensuring the success of digitalization and IT projects. We act as the customer’s independent partner, project manager, and advisor throughout the entire process – from needs analysis to the realization of benefits within the organization.
